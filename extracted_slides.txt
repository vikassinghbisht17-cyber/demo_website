Slide 5:  CHAPTER TWO · DISTRIBUTED TENDER DATA ENGINEERING  Empowering business with hidden gems of data 04 04 Tender Data Lake Pipeline Government procurement portals publish tenders across hundreds of fragmented websites. Passageway’s distributed scraping fleet captures, cleans, and structures this data into a tiered data lake — delivering targeted tender intelligence to subscribers at scale.   What is it? A worker-based, queue-driven architecture that scrapes tender data from 200+ government procurement portals, ingests documents with content-addressed deduplication, and lands structured data in a Bronze–Silver–Gold data lake.  Powered by RabbitMQ, MinIO, Postgres, and Redis. Designed to scale from 30K to 750K tenders per day without redesign. PIPELINE STAGES  Scraping → listing & detail parsing  Document fetching → text extraction  Validation & reconciliation   Distributed Scraper Fleet 7 specialised workers connected by durable queues. Session management via Redis with epoch-based invalidation. Per-site rate limiting and CAPTCHA handling.   Bronze–Silver–Gold Lake Raw captures preserved immutably. Cleaned, deduplicated data in Parquet/Iceberg. Star schema models for analytics and subscriber APIs.   Adapter Registry YAML-driven site configs so adding a portal needs no code deploy. Fixture-based regression testing and canary promotions. Scaling from 200 to 5,000+ procurement portals

Powered by XGBoost, Random Forest, and Logistic Regression. Validated against real conversion outcomes. Deployed at enterprise scale.
